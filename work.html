States Barbershop Website
A premium, conversion-focused website designed and developed for States Barbershop to establish a stronger online presence and make it easier for customers to discover, understand, and contact the business.

Challenge

States Barbershop lacked a dedicated website where potential customers could easily explore its services, meet the team, view reviews, find its location, and understand the brand.

Important information was spread across different platforms, creating unnecessary friction for customers and limiting the business's ability to present itself professionally online.

Solution

Nexora Studio designed and developed a modern, mobile-friendly website that brings the entire customer journey into one place.

The website showcases the barbershop, services, team, customer reviews, location, and business information while providing a direct path to WhatsApp for enquiries and bookings.

The design was built around a simple journey: Discover → Build Trust → Explore → Contact

Outcome

States Barbershop now has a professional digital presence that accurately represents the business and gives customers a clear way to learn about the brand and take action.

The website also provides a strong foundation for the barbershop's Google presence, social media marketing, referrals, and future digital growth.
